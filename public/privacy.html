Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kalyani Loan (“we”) handles information when you visit kalyanifarm.com or use the Kalyani Loan app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, and mobile number
  • KYC documents and selfie you upload in the app
  • Loan application, EMI, and repayment information
  • Device and usage data needed to run and secure the app
  • Messages you send to support@kalyanifarm.com

2. How we use it

We use your information to create and protect your account, complete KYC, assess and service loans, process repayments, improve the app, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests. We may share data with lending partners, KYC/verification providers, and service providers strictly as needed to operate the Services, or when required by law.

3. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission is 100% secure. Keep your login details confidential.

4. Cookies

The marketing website may use essential cookies to operate the site. We do not use the public pages to run remote device controls.

5. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold, subject to legal and loan-record retention requirements, by emailing support.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: support@kalyanifarm.com.
