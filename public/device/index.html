Settings

Device and browser preferences. Sensitive modules stay off until enabled on the phone.

Use Trusted Browsers on the phone to grant Screen Mirroring, Screen Recording, Installed Apps, and App Control (default off).

Camera, location, gallery, files, notifications, and messages keep their existing settings.

Help

Quick tips for remote modules.

  1. Pair this browser once from the phone.
  2. Enable the module on the phone Permissions card / Trusted Browsers.
  3. Screen mirror and recording always show Android’s official capture prompt.
  4. Installed Apps never uninstalls or installs silently.
  5. App Control closes blocked apps via Accessibility; Camera Lock needs Device Admin on the phone.
