How to use

Checking pairing status…

  1. Pair this browser — create a code, scan QR or enter it in the Android app.
  2. My phones — open your phone, choose camera/mic, tap Connect.
  3. Approve on phone — tap Approve in the notification / app screen.
  4. Live view — watch and control from this website.
  5. End anytime — End on website, or Stop / Disconnect on the phone.

Help & safety

  • Camera/mic never start until you tap Approve on the phone.
  • Live video uses WebRTC. Frames are not stored in Firestore.
  • Photos/recordings save on the phone only when you capture/record on purpose.
  • Pairing codes expire in 5 minutes and can be used once.
  • Session requests expire in about 2 minutes if not approved.
  • If live video fails on some networks, configure TURN on the server.
