FAQs

Answers to common questions about the Kalyani Loan app.

Who can apply for a Kalyani Loan?

Indian residents who can complete in-app registration and KYC may apply, subject to eligibility and verification.

How much can I borrow?

You can apply for a personal loan of up to ₹2,00,000 in the app, depending on your profile and review.

How do interest rates work?

Rates are shown in the app before you submit. Lower amounts may have a higher rate; larger approved amounts can qualify for a lower slab.

How do I repay?

Open the Kalyani Loan app, go to EMI / repayment, transfer the amount to the listed bank details, and submit your payment proof.

Is my data safe?

We use account login and encrypted connections. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for details.
