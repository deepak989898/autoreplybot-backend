About Us

Your loan partner, always

Kalyani Loan is built to make personal credit simple: apply on your phone, complete KYC, and manage EMIs without paperwork.

Kalyani Loan is a digital lending experience designed for people who need funds quickly and want a clear process. Download the app, create an account, verify your identity, and apply for a loan of up to ₹2,00,000.

What we stand for

  • Transparent amounts, tenure, and EMI before you submit
  • Paperless KYC inside the app
  • Secure handling of your application and documents
  • Support when you need help with your loan or account

Loans are subject to eligibility, verification, and partner NBFC policies. The app will show your application status — in review, approved, or updates on repayment.

Questions? Write to support@kalyanifarm.com.
